STC Financial Performance Dashboard

Independent educational and portfolio project analyzing Saudi Telecom Company (stc) consolidated financial performance for FY2021–FY2025. The project is designed for an accounting / financial analysis portfolio and is not an official stc product, an assurance engagement, or investment advice.

Business objective

The data pack prepares verified revenue, profitability, financial-position and cash-flow measures for an interactive Power BI dashboard and a Board-level financial performance report. It supports trend analysis, ratio analysis, traceable financial interpretation, and management discussion without inventing causes for observed movements.

Dashboard-ready content

The Excel workbook contains reported source data, a normalized fact table (tblFinancialData), transparent KPI calculations, validation checks, a data dictionary and source mapping. KPI definitions include revenue growth, gross / operating / net margins, current ratio, debt ratio, liabilities-to-equity, closing-balance ROA and ROE, and cash-flow measures.

Financial methodology

Reported consolidated net profit is used. In 2024 and 2025 the consolidated statement includes discontinued operations, so the workbook does not silently substitute continuing-operations profit. Growth is blank / N/A for 2021 because no 2020 baseline was added. ROA and ROE use closing total assets and equity because verified 2020 opening balances were not included. Free cash flow is omitted because capital expenditure is not isolated consistently across all five years.

The Board report presents values in SAR millions for readability, while the underlying data model stores SAR thousands. The report distinguishes reported values, calculated KPIs, analyst interpretation and documented limitations.

Board-level review priorities

The report recommends that management:

•
Reconcile continuing and discontinued operations when assessing recurring profitability.

•
Explain the 2025 net-profit movement through a bridge from operating profit to consolidated net profit.

•
Connect investing and financing cash flows with balance-sheet movements and liquidity planning.

•
Maintain a controlled KPI dictionary distinguishing reported figures from calculated metrics.

•
Validate the final Power BI model, slicers, refresh behavior and DAX outputs against the Excel control totals.

Limitations

The 2024 current-assets comparative follows the 2025 comparative presentation and is flagged in the workbook. Figures are consolidated Group figures. Average-balance ROA and ROE are not used because verified 2020 opening balances were not included. Free cash flow is not shown because comparable capital expenditure data is not isolated consistently across all five years. Reported results are not investment advice.
